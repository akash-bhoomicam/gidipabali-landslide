How we read it

What a sudden radar drop means

A drop of 3 dB means about half the signal is gone. The surface changed between two passes: trees lost, or ground covered by mud or water. Radar alone cannot separate mud from water, so we use terrain, drainage size and optical images too.

Does the signal stand out?

Steps

  1. Radar Six Sentinel-1 passes, terrain corrected, 10 m. Bad radar angles removed.
  2. Change 30 Oct against five earlier passes, with wide-area wetness removed.
  3. Terrain DEM slope, drainage, catchment and height above stream sort slope failure from flood.
  4. Optical Sentinel-2 and PlanetScope quick views, including the 1 Nov cloud gaps.
  5. Exposure 51,418 building footprints and road sections tested against each hazard.
